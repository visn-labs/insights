A LITTLE CLARITY GOES A LONG WAY

Your contribution.
Your decision.

A useful network starts with people who understand what they’re sharing.

What am I contributing?

A CCTV recording you are authorised to share. You choose the camera context and a segment to review. This preview saves the draft details on your device; it does not publish or upload your video.

How would I earn rewards?

Visn’s contributor model is designed around rewards for accepted footage. Reward amounts, review criteria and payout methods have not been finalised. No balance or payout is created by this preview.

Who uses the knowledge base?

The second side of Visn is for people who pay to get information from the insights knowledge base. This experience focuses on helping footage contributors get started.

Do I need to connect a live camera?

No. Start with a recorded clip. We do not ask for camera passwords or open a live connection during onboarding.
