Where is my recording stored?

You select it from your device for a local browser preview. The draft stores metadata in this browser, not the video. After a reload, reselect the recording to preview or revise its interval.

Has it been uploaded or protected?

No. Saving a draft does not upload, publish, blur or redact your recording. This story is an illustration of recording and transmission.

Are rewards active?

No. Submission, acceptance criteria, reward rates and payment methods are planned. A draft does not create earnings.
